# L01 · Introducing yourself

- Status: completed

## Learning objectives

- Give a name, location, and simple purpose.

## Learning content

A short introduction can follow three moves: greeting, identity, and one useful detail. Use “I am” or “I’m” before a name or adjective, and use “I’m from …” for origin. A question such as “What about you?” returns the turn.

## Practice

Write a three-sentence introduction and ask one question.

## Learner submission

Hello, I’m Mina. I from Seoul and I am here for a design workshop. What about you?

## Feedback

The message is clear. Add the missing verb: “I’m from Seoul.” The final question keeps the conversation moving.
