# L04 · Focus and depth of field

- Status: completed

## Learning objectives

- Place focus deliberately.
- Explain how aperture, distance, and focal length affect depth of field.

## Learning content

Focus tells the viewer what must be sharp. A wider aperture such as f/2.8 often gives shallower depth of field than f/8, but camera-to-subject distance and focal length also matter. Moving closer usually makes the focused zone feel thinner. For a portrait, focus on the nearest eye; for a layered scene, decide whether separation or front-to-back detail serves the story.

## Practice

Photograph the same foreground object at f/2.8 and f/8 without changing position. Predict and then compare the background.
