# L07 · Five-photo story

- Status: completed

## Learning objectives

- Create a sequence with beginning, development, detail, action, and ending.
- Apply exposure, composition, light, focus, motion, and colour intentionally.

## Learning content

A photo story needs progression, not seven unrelated strong frames. Establish the place, introduce the subject, show a meaningful detail, include an action, and close with a change or resolution. Visual consistency helps the sequence feel intentional; repetition and variation create rhythm.

## Practice

Create and order five photographs about a morning bicycle commute. Add one sentence explaining each frame’s role.

## Learner submission

1 establishes the quiet street; 2 introduces the rider; 3 shows hands fastening the helmet; 4 uses panning blur during departure; 5 ends with the empty bike rack at work.

## Feedback

The sequence has a clear arc and uses a detail and motion frame for variety. Frame 1 is slightly underexposed, but the overall set is coherent. The final work demonstrates intentional visual choices rather than accidental settings.

## Adaptation

Sprint complete. A next Sprint could focus on editing consistency and stronger environmental portraits.
