# L02 · Composition and visual attention

- Status: completed

## Learning objectives

- Use subject placement, leading lines, and background control.

## Learning content

Composition directs attention. Start by naming the subject, then inspect the frame edges and background for competing elements. The rule of thirds is a useful prompt, not a law. Leading lines work only when they lead toward the subject. Moving a step left or lowering the camera often changes a frame more than changing settings.

## Practice

Make three versions of one scene: centered, off-center, and with a leading line. Choose one and explain why.
