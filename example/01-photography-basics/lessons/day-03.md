# L03 · Direction and quality of light

- Status: completed

## Learning objectives

- Recognize front, side, and back light.
- Preserve highlight detail in contrasty scenes.

## Learning content

Front light makes exposure predictable but can flatten texture. Side light reveals form because one side is brighter than the other. Back light can separate a subject from the background, but bright areas may clip. Soft light creates gradual transitions; hard light creates sharp shadows. Judge light by direction, contrast, and what detail matters most.

## Practice

Photograph one object with front, side, and back light. Compare texture, shadow, and highlight detail.
